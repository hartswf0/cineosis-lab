CAN LABEL ENCODERa drink can, 66 x 122 mm, read by one camera

WHERE THIS BREAKS

Plain unprinted can: no print, no twist reading. Position and tilt still work.

Label that repeats around the can: twist is known only inside one repeat.

Glare: print under the bright stripe is dropped. A wide stripe leaves too little print.

Fast motion: blur removes print. Enrolment needs under 12 deg per frame.

The hand is removed by its colour. Print in the same colour as the hand is lost with it.

The outline comes from background difference. Anything else that moves, or an exposure change, spoils it.

A can leaning toward or away from the camera is not modelled.

A rigid can has one twist. Top third and bottom third are the same angle read twice. Where you hold the can chooses which ring that angle drives.
