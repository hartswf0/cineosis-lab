The claimEach studio bets that its way of working makes the better film of WYGWYL. It makes the whole film its own way, all 14 poems, and no other studio touches it.
The stakeWhat a bet costs: minutes you spend in the studio and decisions you make. A bet that makes a good film cheaply beats one that makes it expensively.
The payoffIn the screening room every film plays the same moment on one voice. Award each poem to the bet that made it best. The winners make the house cut. Every film, and the house cut, exports as one EDL format.
