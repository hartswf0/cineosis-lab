How the chemistry works

atomsthe 45 signs, placed as on Deamer's table. The group is the image type; the period is composition or genesis. Movement-images and time-images are two regimes.
valencea sign of composition holds 2 cuts (one before, one after). A sign of genesis holds 3, so it can branch. Opsign and sonsign hold 1, because a pure situation ends a chain. The lectosign holds 4.
− singlea cut
= doubleshared time: a dissolve or a superimposition. It costs 2 and holds only within one regime.
≡ rhymea match cut between two signs of one image type
⇌ resonancetwo signs a shot can flip between (the confusion table), so the image sits on a hinge
→ breaka movement-image going over into a time-image: the sensory-motor break
··· loosean association with no cut: it costs nothing and never plays
ligandssound elements that bind IN, OFF or OUT of the world. Her voice (Po) binds OUT and is never cut.
reactionsflip an atom into a neighbour, under the condition the table gives. Condense two signs of composition into their genesis. Break a movement-image with a sound move (the catalyst) into an opsign or sonsign; the bonds it can no longer hold turn loose. Crystallise a pure situation into a hyalosign. Read: add the lectosign.
pTtime pressure: 0 is pure movement-image and 14 is pure time-image
synthesisthe compound laid over the poem's lines, each atom given a shot that carries its sign. You can screen it, send it to the Studio as a branch, add it to the CHEM film, or export an EDL.
