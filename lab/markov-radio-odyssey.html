Cool Radio Odyssey

Homer's Odyssey in twenty-four stations. Each book plays as a radio film: the voices of the scenes keep the time, footage from the Moving Image Archive is composed along them, the book's own music lies beneath, and the archive's own sound comes through in the gaps. No need to know the story: each book begins by telling you where you are.

Voices, music (Bronze Council, Waking Son) and the scene script from the Odyssey halfworld film. Footage: movingimagearchive.com, foraged and composed by the Markov Poet.
