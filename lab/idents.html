Cineosis Lab · Idents

Sixty-two idents

A short for each of the 17 films and each of the 45 signs. In every one the mark comes down on its thread holding its number or symbol, then opens its eye onto archive shots and cuts between them by blinking. It holds on one shot and winks at it, then names itself.

Colour lives in the OEach film has its own colour; each sign takes its family's colour from the periodic table. It appears only inside the O, as the tone of the archive shots. Outside the O there is only paper, ink and the red thread.
FigureWhat tells them apart at a glance is the figure: how many Os, where they sit, how big, and the path the red thread takes. Nine eyes around one O for the machines, three Os racing for choosing, a voyage thread for the Odyssey, a broken O for a break.
RhythmThe cuts keep the film's own time. Choosing cuts fast, Drift slows down, the voice cuts on an uneven beat.
Blink and winkEvery cut is a blink. The one shot the ident holds is winked at. Red is only the thread, and nothing is drawn over a picture.
