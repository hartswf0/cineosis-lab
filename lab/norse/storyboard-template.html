Cineosis Lab · for the Norse Half Moon film

Storyboard your film from your own voice.

Your voice memo has been split into its lines, one panel for each, in your seven chapters. For every line the archive has already picked pictures and cut-out figures that fit. You choose, and place them. No experience needed: nothing you do can break anything, and it saves as you go.

1Pick a picture

Each line comes with twelve archive pictures that fit it. Tap one to set the scene.

2Add and drag figures

Tap a figure to put it in the panel, then drag it where it belongs. Make it bigger, smaller, or turn it around.

3Hear it, show it

Play the board and it turns, panel by panel, in time with your own voice. Print it as a storyboard sheet, or share a link.

Works on a phone or a laptop. Your board stays in this browser; use Share to send it to someone or open it on another device.
