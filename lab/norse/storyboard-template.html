Cineosis Lab · for the Norse Half Moon film

Shape the film like a sculptor, with moving pictures.

Start from the rough block of your voice memo, or from nothing. Write each shot in your own words, the way you would say it to a camera operator, and the archive finds moving pictures and figures for it, leaning toward the house: the domes, the carving, the coast. Keep what is true, cut what is not, add what is missing. Nothing you do can break anything, and it saves as you go.

Fog lifts off the domes at dawn

1Write a shot

Say what the camera sees, in plain words. The panel finds moving pictures for your words as you type, and shows which words it understood.

2Shape it

Choose another picture, add people and things cut out of old films, drag them into place, make them bigger or smaller.

3Carve the story

Move shots earlier or later, cut what does not belong, add what is missing. Play it back, print the storyboard, or share a link.
