# FOLLOW — one character, one colour, one run of pickups

Why: the atlas counts how often each character's colour is in frame across the 270 AI shots — **orange 37 (mostly pails), purple 12, yellow 6**; the black slab is barely there; points of view: **optic 2, face 10, witness 15** against 212 medium/embedded. These runs fill those holes, and each follows ONE colour so a film can be cut from its eyes.

Every prompt = the LOCK text from bible.json (pails / cook / crew / four / world) + the run's line below + the NEG list. Each patch is 6–12 s (CUTBASTARD), and names its trigger so the cut has a reason.

## PURPLE — the tall slab who watches the beach like a dashboard
1. **optic · 10 s · trigger: threshold.** Through a binocular reticle from the offshore fleet: the beach, the fort, the crew small; the reticle drifts and stops on the flat purple slab standing in the surf among the pails, its tiny white eyes turning to look back up the lens.
2. **witness · 8 s · trigger: entity entry.** Low drifting witness behind the purple slab as it shears forward across wet sand, the crew parting around a flat shape they do not look at; its flat colour never takes the evening light.
3. **face · 8 s · trigger: pressure shift.** Held close-up on the purple slab against the burning fort, white eyes tracking left-right-left with a cursor we cannot see, the surf behind it alive, nothing else moving.

## BLACK — the slab with the big eyes, the one who reads the screen
1. **screen · 8 s · trigger: light.** Inside a login screen at monitor scale: the black slab fills the frame, its big white eyes reflect the beach in the glass; a change request pops up over its face.
2. **embedded · 10 s · trigger: object transfer.** First person as a crew member hands a pail up the line; the black slab stands in the line too, receives it flat (no hands — the pail just attaches to its edge), passes it on.
3. **wide · 12 s · trigger: time jump.** Locked sand-level wide at dusk: the black slab alone on the drained beach among a thousand upturned pails, eyes the only light.

## YELLOW — the capsule whose mouth runs past its edge
1. **face · 8 s · trigger: sound.** Close-up of the yellow capsule as the crew chant "into the mud"; its line mouth extends past its own outline on each beat, flat, no lip-sync.
2. **insert · 6 s · trigger: object transfer.** Macro: the cook's spatula slides a browned burger toward the yellow capsule's line mouth; the burger stops at the flat edge, steam crossing the colour.
3. **witness · 10 s · trigger: kinetic vector.** Witness drifts after the yellow capsule as it bounces (by shear only) between drill rigs pounding sand, landing on each downbeat.

## ORANGE — the half-dome that is the same colour as the pails
1. **wide · 10 s · trigger: entity entry.** Locked wide of the upturned-pail field; one of the "pails" turns out to be the flat orange half-dome, dot eyes opening.
2. **embedded · 8 s · trigger: camera movement.** First person pounding a pail into sand to the beat; on the third stroke the orange half-dome is under the crossbar, flat, unbothered, and the drill passes through it.
3. **optic · 10 s · trigger: threshold.** Viewfinder from the fort wall: the orange half-dome at the waterline as the tide comes in; the water stops at its flat edge.

## THE COOK — the developer who feeds the line (the meme's stare)
1. **face · 12 s · trigger: pressure shift.** The meme's stare, ours: skull mask held in close-up, breathing in the headset, the four flat shapes reflected small in the eye holes' darkness.
2. **witness · 10 s · trigger: location.** Witness follows the cook down the crew line at night, griddle on his left forearm lit by fires, burgers passed hand to hand behind him.
3. **optic · 8 s · trigger: entity exit.** From the fleet's reticle: the cook looks straight up the lens, lifts one burger, and turns away into the smoke.
