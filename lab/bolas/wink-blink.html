The Operative Archive · Cineosis Lab

Wink,
not blink.

A blink and a wink are the same lid moving. A blink is involuntary, both eyes at once, too fast to mean anything. A wink is aimed at someone, held, and means everything. The machine blinks. The reader winks. That difference is the whole of the lab, and the whole of the mark.
