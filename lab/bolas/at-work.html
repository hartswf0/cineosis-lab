Cineosis Lab · Bolas

Bolas at Work

The agent that performs each film. The body becomes the operation: a reel, a lens, a shutter, a countdown, a page. The head with its two eyes hangs beneath, and the eight legs make the work visible. One small looping scene for every film and every sign, legible from across a room.

Thread
Red, and only ever the thread. It is how Bolas arrives and what it pulls.
Body
An O that turns into the operation of the film: reel, iris, shutter, counter, waveform, grid, page. Colour lives only here.
Head
Below the body: a small O with two eyes. It blinks when something passes and winks when it has read something.
Legs
Eight jointed lines. They hold, pull, tap, weave, cut and carry, so you can see what the film does from far off.
Props
Line, circle, dot: frames, strips, lanes, beads, nodes. Nothing else.

The wall

All 62 scenes at once, as they would sit on a contact sheet or a lobby screen. If a scene cannot be told apart at this size, it does not work yet.

tileground

The films

Seventeen scenes, one per film, each showing what that film does.

The signs

Forty-five scenes. The body is the sign's tile, holding its symbol, with the family's colour inside the O. The legs act out the family: sight lines for perception, a close face for affect, force for action, a twin for reflection, a web for the mental image, turning time, a snapped thread for the break, a page for the readable image.

Teams

Bolas working together. In the line, a shot travels along the thread and each Bolas does one operation to it before passing it on. In the chorus, a blink runs down the row like a machine pass, and one of them winks.

Beside the idents

Each film and sign ident, next to the Bolas scene that does its work. The ident shows the archive; the scene shows the operation.

Everything on this page is drawn live from the same few parts. Red is the thread alone. Colour appears only inside an O. The sign glosses come from the lab's table of signs.
