OPEN CUT

Golf and tennis at once. Everyone sits around one round field of shots and putts in turn, as in golf; but the shot your ball comes to rest on must answer the last shot of the one film you are all making, as a tennis return answers the last hit. The film plays in the middle.

The land follows the film. Seen from the film’s last shot, good cuts are hollows (green) and jarring cuts are hills (red); after every cut the whole land changes. Claim: come to rest on a free shot that cuts well and it joins the film as yours (1 point, 3 for a good cut). Refused: a jarring cut is not taken. Territory: claimed shots are bumpers for everyone. Empty ground and the screen send you back. Putt by pulling back from your ball; hold still to narrow the forecast; tap once mid-roll to burn.
