TENNIS FOR TWO, THROUGH THE ARCHIVE

Tennis for Two, through the archive, as an editor. The court inside the ring is a field of shots. Every return's line of flight is a cut: each shot the ball crosses joins the rally film, for as long as the ball is over it, and belongs to whoever hit it.

Aim by placing your paddle. As the ball comes to you, a dashed line shows the return your paddle would send and lights the shots it would cut, with how well they join the film so far. Hit off-centre to angle the line; move as you hit to add spin. Points: win a rally (your opponent misses), or cut a return that joins well (a mean cut of 60 or more). First to 7. Move: drag around the ring (two here: left half blue, right half red), or keys A/D and the arrows.
