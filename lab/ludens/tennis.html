TENNIS FOR TWO, THROUGH THE ARCHIVE

A ring of shots from the Slopfeeder field, in the order of meaning. Two paddles ride the ring. Every shot you return is the next cut of the rally, and where you send the ball decides which shot your opponent has to cut to next.

The cut is the physics. A return that makes a good cut from the last shot comes off soft. A jarring cut comes back hot, faster than you’d like. Miss and your opponent takes the point; first to 7. Each player keeps the shots they hit; the rally is a film.
Move: drag around the ring (on two-here, the left half of the screen is blue and the right half is red), or keys A/D and ←/→. Hit off-centre to angle the return; move as you hit to add spin.
