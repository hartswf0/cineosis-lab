PUTT-OFF

Editing as golf. The field is 1,469 Slopfeeder shots laid out by meaning. Putt your ball across it: wherever it comes to rest is the next shot of your film. The flag is a shot from the story the hole must end on.

The land is the critic. Seen from your last shot, a good cut is a hollow (the ball settles in it, the cell glows green), a jarring cut is a hill (it pushes the ball off, red). Empty ground is water: back to your last lie, one stroke. A jarring cut costs a stroke.
Putt: pull back from the ball and let go. Forecast: hold still while aiming and the cloud of possible putts narrows (as in PUTT-OP). Burn: tap once while the ball rolls to nudge it toward the tap. Rewind: undo your last stroke for one stroke.
