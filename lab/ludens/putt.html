THE COURSE

Editing as golf. Each hole is a chapter of the Slopfeeder films: its own field of shots, its flag a shot the story stands on. Putt across it, and wherever your ball comes to rest is the next shot of your film. Pick the chapters you want to play.

The land is the data (LAND in the header): good cuts from your last shot are hollows, the story's shots are deep wells, poison is a ridge, shots that lead to many good cuts sit lower. Empty ground is water: back to your lie, one stroke. A jarring cut costs a stroke. Putt: pull back and let go. Forecast: hold still while aiming and the cloud narrows. Burn: tap once while it rolls. Rewind: undo a stroke, for a stroke. Change LAYOUT and LENS in the header at any time.
