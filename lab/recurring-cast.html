THE RECURRING CAST

Who the archive keeps sending back. Every archive film, clip and voice used across the lab's films, counted by the job it does there: seen as a picture, heard answering in its own voice, lending single words to a spoken mosaic, cast as a cut-out character, used as a place, or playing a named character's voice. A source film stands in for "who": no faces are recognised or named.

    WHAT THE IMAGES DO

    Every film asks its pictures for almost the same job: match the words. The picker finds the clip nearest to a spoken line or a named thing, so the same shot becomes whatever the sentence needs. Only WYGWYL asks pictures for an editing operation (hold, split, inset, dissolve, montage).
