Cineosis Lab

Deleuze's 44 cinematic signs, after Deamer: a periodic table to read them, a lab of archive shots to find them, and films cut from those shots.

On this site the editors use the 335 shots that ship with it; any archive shot, live archive search and saved sub-shots need your own machine: git clone https://github.com/hartswf0/cineosis-lab, then python3 lab/server.py 8765, then open localhost:8765.

Readings are one editor's judgements; the machine's sign affinity is a pointer, not a reading. After David Deamer, Deleuze's Cinema Books: Three Introductions to the Taxonomy of Images (Edinburgh UP, 2016). Shots from movingimagearchive.com; each links back to its source.
